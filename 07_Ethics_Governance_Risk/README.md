# 07 Ethics, Governance, and Risk

This folder defines the Responsible AI controls, enterprise product risks, privacy and data governance, launch and commissioning gates, incident response, fallback and rollback, and governance responsibilities for the Enterprise AI Risk Governance and Assurance Framework.

## Files

| File | Purpose |

| `02_Product_Risk_Register.md` | Enterprise risk method, risk tiers, core risks, required fields, and review frequency |
| `03_Privacy_and_Data_Governance.md` | Data categories, lifecycle controls, access, supplier governance, monitoring, and incidents |
| `04_Launch_Blockers_and_Decision_Gates.md` | Seven-stage gates, mandatory blockers, decisions, exceptions, and revalidation triggers |

## Scope

Automotive AI, ECU, ADAS, connected-vehicle, OTA, fleet, and supplier risks are the primary scope. Wind-turbine predictive-maintenance, sensor, wake-steering, control, and operational risks form the secondary scope.

## Governance Position

RiskReview AI provides evidence-linked decision support. Authorised humans retain responsibility for risk scoring, control approval, exceptions, residual-risk acceptance, launch, commissioning, restriction, rollback, and closure.

## Evidence Position

Risk scores, residual targets, risk-reduction estimates, financial impacts, loss avoidance, ROI, and payback are scenario values until supported by controlled-pilot or organisational evidence.
