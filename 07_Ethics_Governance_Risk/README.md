# Ethics, Governance and Risk

This folder defines the responsible-AI, privacy, security, operational-risk and launch-control requirements for RiskReview AI.

RiskReview AI is a human-supervised assistant for programme-level risk-register quality review in Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive component New Product Development and New Product Introduction programmes, from APQP to Start of Production.

## Artifacts

1. `01_Responsible_AI.md` - responsible-AI principles and product controls
2. `02_Product_Risk_Register.md` - product, AI, operational and adoption risks
3. `03_Privacy_and_Data_Governance.md` - data minimisation, access, retention and confidentiality
4. `04_Launch_Blockers_and_Decision_Gates.md` - conditions that block or permit progression
5. `05_Incident_Response_Fallback_Rollback.md` - incident handling, fallback and recovery
6. `06_Governance_RACI.md` - accountability across the product lifecycle

All thresholds are provisional until validated using the approved synthetic evaluation set and target-user testing.
