# Launch Blockers and Decision Gates

## Decision Outcomes

At every gate, Team004 or the authorised sponsor selects one of the following:

- Proceed
- Proceed with conditions
- Rework
- Pause
- Stop

## Gate 1: Proposal to Prototype

Proceed only when:

- Industry, segment, user and workflow are specific
- MVP boundaries are documented
- Synthetic-data plan is approved
- Human accountability is explicit
- Prohibited uses are documented

## Gate 2: Prototype to Controlled Usability Test

Proceed only when:

- All outputs are clearly labelled synthetic or simulated
- Original and suggested text appear together
- Every AI finding requires a human decision
- Audit records are captured
- Manual fallback is available
- Severe prohibited actions are absent

## Gate 3: Usability Test to Controlled Pilot

Provisional criteria:

- Meaning-change rate is below the approved threshold
- Unsupported-fact rate is below the approved threshold
- Related-risk suggestions meet approved precision
- Reviewers can identify AI versus deterministic findings
- Human correction burden is acceptable
- Security and privacy controls are approved

Exact thresholds remain provisional until the labelled evaluation set and discovery evidence are complete.

## Gate 4: Controlled Pilot to Paid Pilot

Proceed only when:

- A named economic buyer is confirmed
- Customer value is measured against a manual baseline
- Cost-to-serve is understood
- User and sponsor acceptance is demonstrated
- Incident response and rollback are tested
- Contractual and data-governance approvals exist

## Non-Negotiable Launch Blockers

The product must not progress if:

1. It frequently changes the intended meaning of risks.
2. It fabricates programme facts or evidence.
3. It automatically assigns probability or impact.
4. It automatically approves PPAP or Start of Production.
5. It replaces DFMEA or PFMEA decisions.
6. It automatically merges, closes, accepts or escalates risks.
7. Confidential data cannot be protected.
8. Audit and traceability cannot be maintained.
9. Users cannot correct or reject findings.
10. Manual fallback is unavailable.
11. Performance is materially unfair across workstreams or writing styles.
12. Provider changes cannot be detected through regression testing.

## Change-Control Triggers

Re-evaluation is required after:

- Model, provider or major prompt change
- Rule or threshold change
- New customer taxonomy
- New automotive segment or lifecycle stage
- New integration
- Material incident
- Significant decline in reviewer acceptance or trust
