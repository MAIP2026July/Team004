# Demo Guide

## Suggested five-minute demonstration

1. Open `app/index.html`.
2. On **Dashboard**, show the synthetic programme context and quality findings.
3. Open **Risk finding** and explain the cause-event-impact review.
4. Select **Edit and accept** to demonstrate human control.
5. Open **Related risks** and compare two risks before choosing Link, Merge, Keep Separate or Defer.
6. Open **Mitigation challenge** and show how the product asks for stronger evidence without inventing facts.
7. Open **Review agenda** to show the verified meeting-preparation output.
8. Open **Audit & evaluation** to show decision traceability and clearly labelled illustrative metrics.

## Demonstration statement

> This is a clickable workflow prototype using synthetic automotive programme-risk data and simulated AI findings. It demonstrates the proposed user experience and human controls, not production model accuracy or business outcomes.
