# RiskReview AI Prototype Demo Guide

## Demo objective
Show how the expanded enterprise platform converts risk records into traceable AI recommendations while preserving final human authority. Use the included synthetic workbook only.

## Suggested 8–10 minute walkthrough
1. **Open the app:** double-click `App/index.html`.
2. **Upload:** select `OEM_Enterprise_AI_Risk_Register-v6.xlsx`. Explain the mandatory-field, 1–5 score-range and evidence checks.
3. **Quality Dashboard:** show 10 synthetic risks spanning automotive AI, wind energy and enterprise governance.
4. **Risk Finding:** open AUTO-OTA-001. Explain score 15 = likelihood 3 × impact 5 and the evidence-linked recommendation.
5. **Related Risks:** compare RISK-112 and RISK-145. Select Link rather than Merge because ownership and impacts differ.
6. **Mitigation Challenge:** replace vague supplier language with a measurable owner, date, sampling rule and acceptance threshold.
7. **Human Decision:** choose Escalate for ADAS-VIS-002 and retain the reason, role, evidence and version.
8. **Verified Agenda:** show that only reviewed findings enter governance.
9. **Evaluation:** distinguish finding precision and traceability from human acceptance.

## Core message
RiskReview AI detects potential gaps, explains recommendations and structures governance. Authorised humans decide whether to accept, modify, reject or escalate.

## Reset
Refresh the browser or select Upload in the left navigation. No information is saved beyond the browser session.
