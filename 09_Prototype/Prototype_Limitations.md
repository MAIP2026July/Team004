# Prototype Limitations

- Static front-end demonstration; no backend, database, identity provider or role enforcement.
- File selection demonstrates UX only; the browser does not parse the workbook.
- All risks, evidence references, names, dates and evaluation measures are synthetic.
- No connection to OEM, supplier, vehicle, SCADA, Product Lifecycle Management, Security Information and Event Management or regulatory systems.
- No production Large Language Model call, Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline, vector database or model monitoring service.
- The prototype does not establish compliance with ISO 26262, ISO/SAE 21434, Automotive SPICE, UNECE R155/R156, the EU AI Act or any jurisdiction-specific law.
- Explainable AI is represented as a governance requirement; SHAP, LIME and Grad-CAM are not executed.
- Risk bands are demonstration defaults and require enterprise approval.
- Evaluation uses a small synthetic set and cannot support safety, accuracy, ROI, certification or deployment claims.
- AI cannot approve safety-critical release, supplier acceptance, legal compliance, risk closure, production launch or investment.

## Production work required
Secure architecture, threat modelling, privacy assessment, data contracts, access control, audit immutability, model validation, prompt-injection testing, human-factors testing, integration testing, disaster recovery, monitoring and independent assurance are required before deployment.
