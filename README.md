Initial workflow: Monthly programme-risk and launch-readiness review
Product Summary
RiskReview AI is a human-supervised AI copilot that reviews the quality and readiness of automotive programme risk registers before monthly launch-readiness meetings.

The product identifies incomplete risk entries, weak cause-event-impact structure, potentially related risks, stale evidence and unclear mitigation actions. All probability, impact, acceptance, escalation, merging and closure decisions remain with authorised project personnel.
