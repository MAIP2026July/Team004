# Business, Economics and Scaling

This folder explains how RiskReview AI can create measurable customer value, control cost-to-serve, progress from an MVP to a paid pilot, and scale responsibly within the selected automotive component NPD/NPI segment.

The artifacts cover:

- Customer and business value
- Baseline and benefits measurement
- Cost-to-serve and unit economics
- Pricing and commercial model
- MVP-to-first-revenue plan
- Scaling constraints and operating model
- Assumptions that require validation

All financial values in this folder are illustrative until validated through interviews, pilot evidence and buyer discussions.
