## Commercial Position

RiskReview AI is an enterprise governance and assurance product. It supports, but does not replace, human risk acceptance, safety, quality, cybersecurity, regulatory, launch, or commissioning decisions.

## Evidence Position

The ₹10 crore investment, 67% risk-reduction expectation, ₹710 crore annualised loss-avoidance estimate, 7,000% indicative net ROI, sub-one-week payback, operating-cost range, and pricing ranges are planning scenarios. These figures are not achieved outcomes and require controlled-pilot, financial, and customer validation.

## Recommended Starting Point

Start with one controlled automotive AI or ECU programme, establish the baseline, run the model in shadow mode, measure technical and workflow outcomes, update unit economics, and move to an annual contract only when the evidence supports the next investment gate.
