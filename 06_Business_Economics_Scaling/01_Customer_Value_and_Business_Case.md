# Customer Value and Business Case

## 1. Business Need

Enterprises deploying Artificial Intelligence in automotive Electronic Control Units, Advanced Driver Assistance Systems, connected vehicles, Over-the-Air updates, supplier platforms, and wind-turbine operations face model, data, cybersecurity, supplier, operational, financial, and governance risks.

Risk evidence is often distributed across engineering reports, supplier documents, validation logs, cybersecurity findings, operational telemetry, risk registers, and approval records. This fragmentation can increase manual review effort, repeated corrections, validation delays, and the probability that material evidence gaps remain unresolved.

## 2. Customer Segments

### Primary Segment

- Automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive component suppliers
- Automotive AI, ECU, ADAS, connected-vehicle, and OTA programme teams

### Secondary Segment

- Wind-turbine manufacturers
- Wind-farm operators
- Renewable-energy asset owners
- Predictive-maintenance and operational-control teams

## 3. Users, Buyers, and Beneficiaries

| Role | Primary Need | Expected Value |
|---|---|---|
| Product or Programme Leader | Decision-ready view of material AI risks | Earlier visibility of launch and commissioning blockers |
| AI or ECU Engineer | Traceable model, data, and validation evidence | More focused investigation and revalidation |
| Safety, Quality, and Cybersecurity Reviewer | Evidence-linked findings and control status | Consistent challenge and approval records |
| Operations or Maintenance Manager | Explainable alerts and operating limits | Better inspection, restriction, or monitoring decisions |
| Procurement and Supplier Quality | Supplier transparency and change evidence | Reduced black-box and dependency exposure |
| Executive Risk Authority | Consolidated inherent and residual risk | Defensible Go/No-Go and exception decisions |
| Internal Audit | Traceable human and model actions | Improved auditability and control testing |

## 4. Value Proposition

RiskReview AI combines deterministic validation, model-assisted analysis, Human-in-the-Loop decisions, governance gates, audit trails, and continuous monitoring.

The framework is expected to help customers:

- Reduce manual preparation for risk, launch, and commissioning reviews
- Reduce repeated correction of incomplete or inconsistent records
- Identify missing, stale, or weak evidence earlier
- Connect related risks across product, supplier, cybersecurity, and operations
- Improve Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier transparency
- Maintain traceable human acceptance, rejection, modification, and escalation decisions
- Detect model, data, sensor, or operating-condition drift after deployment
- Reduce avoidable delay, rework, disruption, and associated review cost

These outcomes remain hypotheses until measured through customer discovery and controlled pilots.

## 5. Business-Case Logic

### Annual Benefit Components

**Expected Annual Benefit = Review Efficiency + Avoided Rework + Avoided Delay + Risk-Adjusted Incident Avoidance + Audit and Compliance Efficiency**

Where:

- **Review Efficiency** = Hours saved × fully loaded hourly cost
- **Avoided Rework** = Avoided engineering or operational rework hours × hourly cost
- **Avoided Delay** = Avoided delay days × estimated cost per day
- **Risk-Adjusted Incident Avoidance** = Incident probability reduction × financial consequence
- **Audit Efficiency** = Reduced evidence-collection hours × hourly cost

### Net Benefit

**Net Annual Benefit = Expected Annual Benefit − Annual Operating Cost**

### Return on Investment

**Net ROI % = (Annual Benefit − Annual Cost) ÷ Annual Cost × 100**

### Payback

**Payback Period = Initial Investment ÷ Monthly Net Benefit**

## 6. Current Scenario

The proposed programme assumes a total platform investment of **₹10 crore**, equal to **₹1,000 lakh**, across six core modules and a seven-stage rollout.

The current risk model indicates a possible reduction in average exposure from **17.2 Critical** to approximately **5.6 Medium**, or about **67%**, and identifies six launch-blocking Critical risks that the proposed controls are intended to address.

The financial scenario estimates up to **₹710 crore in annualised loss avoidance**, an indicative **7,000% net ROI**, and payback below one week. These figures are scenario-based projections, not achieved results. The projections depend on incident probabilities, recall or disruption consequences, mitigation effectiveness, adoption, and the treatment of avoided loss as economic benefit.

## 7. Evidence Required Before Approval

Before presenting the scenario as a validated business case, the team must obtain:

- Baseline review hours and rework hours
- Fully loaded cost per user group
- Historical delay and disruption evidence
- Incident probability and consequence evidence
- Cost of recall, warranty, outage, maintenance dispatch, and regulatory response
- Pilot performance and human override results
- Actual infrastructure, integration, support, and assurance costs
- Sensitivity analysis for best, expected, and downside cases

## 8. Recommended Decision

Proceed with a controlled, time-bounded pilot rather than committing immediately to global scale. The pilot should test one automotive AI or ECU programme first, establish baseline data, measure workflow improvement, validate governance controls, and update the business case before the next investment gate.
