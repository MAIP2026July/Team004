# Customer Value and Business Case

## Product Context

RiskReview AI supports Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive component manufacturers conducting monthly programme-risk and launch-readiness reviews from APQP to Start of Production.

## Customer Problem

Programme risk registers are maintained by different workstream owners. Entries may contain missing ownership, weak cause-event-impact structure, unclear mitigation, stale evidence or overlapping risks. Review meetings may therefore spend time correcting the register instead of making preventive launch-readiness decisions.

## Primary Value Hypothesis

RiskReview AI can create value by:

1. Reducing pre-meeting manual review effort.
2. Improving completeness and consistency of risk entries.
3. Identifying weak mitigation and stale evidence earlier.
4. Producing a verified review agenda.
5. Preserving traceability of AI findings and human decisions.

## Value Formula

```text
Monthly customer value
= reviewer hours saved × loaded hourly cost
+ avoidable rework reduced
+ improved review readiness
- AI processing cost
- human verification cost
- support and configuration cost
```

## Illustrative Example, Not a Measured Result

Assume a programme uses 12 reviewer-hours per monthly cycle and the controlled MVP saves 3 hours. If the loaded reviewer cost is INR 1,500 per hour, the direct labour value is INR 4,500 per monthly review. This figure excludes avoided rework and decision-readiness value and must not be presented as a validated result.

## Evidence Required

- Current preparation time per review
- Number and seniority of reviewers
- Loaded labour cost approved by the customer
- Rework caused by incomplete risk entries
- Frequency of monthly or weekly reviews
- User acceptance and correction burden
- Willingness to pay for a controlled pilot

## Value Boundaries

RiskReview AI should claim improved preparation efficiency and register quality only when measured. The product should not claim that it prevents programme delay, warranty cost or financial loss without causal evidence.
