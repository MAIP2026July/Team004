# MVP to First Revenue Plan

## Stage 1: Discovery, Weeks 1 to 2

- Interview at least five relevant stakeholders.
- Confirm the monthly review problem and economic buyer.
- Measure the manual baseline.
- Validate data access and confidentiality constraints.

**Gate:** Proceed only if the workflow is repeated, material and accessible for validation.

## Stage 2: Clickable Prototype, Weeks 3 to 4

- Demonstrate register upload, rule checks and AI-assisted findings.
- Demonstrate human accept, edit, reject, link, merge and defer actions.
- Capture usability feedback using synthetic data.

**Gate:** Rework if users cannot understand the findings or evidence.

## Stage 3: Controlled MVP, Months 2 to 3

- Use one approved register format.
- Test with one programme team.
- Compare manual and assisted preparation.
- Measure useful-finding rate, correction burden and severe errors.

**Gate:** Stop or rework if meaning changes, unsupported facts or unsafe automation are observed.

## Stage 4: First Paid Pilot, Months 3 to 6

- Fixed scope and fixed fee.
- One programme and one customer team.
- Human approval for all consequential decisions.
- Agreed data handling, support and success criteria.
- Final pilot evaluation report.

**Commercial target:** Obtain genuine pilot revenue. Exact price remains TBD after buyer research.

## Stage 5: Recurring Contract, Months 6 to 12

- Annual B2B subscription.
- Active-programme or usage tier.
- Customer-specific configuration.
- Monitoring, support, versioning and incident response.

## Expansion Sequence

```text
Automotive component NPD/NPI programme
→ additional programmes in the same customer
→ additional component categories
→ adjacent automotive programme workflows
→ other industries only after separate validation
```
