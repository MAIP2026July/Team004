# MVP to First Revenue Plan

## 1. Objective

Move from the current academic framework and prototype artifacts to the first paid customer engagement without presenting scenario projections as achieved business results.

## 2. Target Beachhead

The recommended first market is one automotive AI, ECU, ADAS, connected-vehicle, or OTA programme within an Original Equipment Manufacturer or Tier 1/Tier 2 supplier.

The beachhead workflow should have:

- A recurring risk or launch-readiness review
- Accessible and approved risk records
- A named product or programme sponsor
- Domain reviewers
- Measurable preparation time and rework
- Clear Human-in-the-Loop authority
- No autonomous operational decision requirement

## 3. Revenue Path

| Stage | Indicative Duration | Key Output | Revenue Position |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Customer discovery | 4–6 weeks | Validated problem, buyer, baseline, and pilot scope | Usually unpaid or low-fee discovery |
| Readiness assessment | 4–6 weeks | Data, workflow, security, governance, and value report | Fixed fee: ₹15–₹30 lakh hypothesis |
| Functional MVP | 8–12 weeks | Risk intake, rules, model findings, human decisions, and audit | Development funded internally or by design partner |
| Shadow-mode pilot | 12–16 weeks | Comparison with expert decisions and measured workflow value | Paid pilot: ₹40–₹80 lakh hypothesis |
| Controlled production | 3–6 months after pilot | Approved deployment, monitoring, support, and renewal criteria | Annual subscription and implementation fee |
| Expansion | After verified value | Additional programmes, sites, fleets, or wind-energy use cases | Upsell or enterprise agreement |

## 4. First-Revenue Offer

The first paid pilot should include:

- One approved workflow and domain
- Defined risk taxonomy and data schema
- Import of an agreed risk dataset
- Deterministic field, date, score, and evidence checks
- Model-assisted cause-event-impact, evidence, and related-risk review
- Human accept, reject, modify, request-evidence, and escalate actions
- Audit history and governance-gate status
- Shadow-mode comparison with expert decisions
- Final technical, governance, workflow, and value report

## 5. Pilot Success Gates

- Named sponsor, buyer, users, and decision authority
- Approved data-access and security process
- Baseline review time and rework measured
- All material model findings receive human disposition
- Critical and High errors individually reviewed
- No autonomous risk acceptance, launch, or commissioning
- User value and willingness to continue documented
- Updated cost-to-serve and commercial proposal produced

## 6. Sales Evidence Pack

- Executive problem statement
- Demonstration of the Human–AI workflow
- Sample synthetic risk register
- Model Card and data strategy
- Security and privacy summary
- Pilot plan and success measures
- Scenario-based business case with sensitivity analysis
- Clear statement that risk reduction and financial projections require validation

## 7. First-Revenue Risks

- Sponsor cannot provide approved data
- Buyer and user belong to different functions
- Pilot becomes an unbounded integration project
- Customer expects safety or regulatory certification
- Small dataset produces unreliable performance claims
- Pilot value is not compared with a baseline
- Contracting, cybersecurity, or supplier approvals exceed the planned timeline

## 8. Decision After Pilot

Proceed to annual contract only if the pilot demonstrates acceptable model quality, effective human oversight, secure data handling, measurable workflow value, manageable cost to serve, and a buyer-approved commercial case.
