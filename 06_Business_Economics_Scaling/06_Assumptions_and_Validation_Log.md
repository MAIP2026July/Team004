## 3. Validation Evidence Levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Internal idea or scenario assumption |
| 1 | Supported by secondary research or expert opinion |
| 2 | Confirmed by multiple customer or user interviews |
| 3 | Demonstrated in prototype or shadow-mode testing |
| 4 | Demonstrated in a controlled paid pilot |
| 5 | Repeated across customers or operating environments |

## 4. Update Rules

- Record the date, source, reviewer, and evidence level for every change.
- Preserve the earlier assumption and reason for revision.
- Separate observed facts from interpretations.
- Do not convert a scenario projection into an achieved result without pilot evidence.
- Recalculate ROI and payback whenever costs, probabilities, or benefits change materially.
- Review Critical commercial assumptions before each funding or scaling gate.
