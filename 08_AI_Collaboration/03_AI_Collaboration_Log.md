# AI Collaboration Log

## 1. Purpose
This log records where generative AI supported the project, what input was supplied, what output was produced, and how the team reviewed the result. It provides transparency without transferring authorship or accountability to the AI system.

## 2. Collaboration Principles
- AI is used for structuring, drafting, comparison, consistency checking, and alternative generation.
- Team members define the problem, scope, evidence, decisions, and final wording.
- AI output is treated as a draft until reviewed.
- Confidential, personal, proprietary, and safety-sensitive information is minimised.
- Unsupported AI claims are removed or labelled as assumptions.

## 5. What AI Did Not Decide
AI did not independently approve the product scope, risk scores, governance thresholds, safety controls, legal compliance, supplier acceptance, production release, investment, or final submission. These decisions remain with the team and the relevant authorised enterprise roles.

## 6. Maintenance Rule
Update this log whenever AI materially changes an artifact, calculation, workflow, requirement, decision, or conclusion. Minor spelling and formatting support may be grouped into one entry. Commit IDs or pull-request links should be added when available.
