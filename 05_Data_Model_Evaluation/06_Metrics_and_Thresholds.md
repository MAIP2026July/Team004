# Metrics and Thresholds

## Provisional prototype targets
- Deterministic checks: 100% on the controlled test cases.
- Related-risk precision: at least 80% on held-out synthetic pairs.
- Human-review agreement on defined quality issues: at least 80%.
- Useful-finding rate: at least 70%.
- Unsupported or fabricated suggestions: below 5%, with severe incidents treated as launch blockers.
- Meaning-changing suggestions: zero severe incidents in the controlled pilot.
- Displayed findings with traceable reason/evidence: 100%.
- Automatic probability, impact, approval, escalation or closure decisions: zero.

## Product and operational measures
Track acceptance, edit, rejection, defer and escalation rates; preparation time; review time per finding; processing time; cost per register; fallback use; and reviewer-reported trust.

## Business measures
Track improvement in register completeness, reduction in meeting-cleaning effort and improvement in review readiness. Do not claim prevention of project loss without evidence.

## Decision rule
Proceed only when value targets and safety guardrails are both satisfied. A high average usefulness score cannot compensate for severe meaning change, fabricated facts or missing traceability.
