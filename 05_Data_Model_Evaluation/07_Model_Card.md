# Model Card

## Product component
AI-assisted semantic review within RiskReview AI.

## Intended use
Support authorised automotive programme personnel by reviewing fictional or approved programme-risk text for structure, related-risk candidates, mitigation questions and meeting-agenda preparation.

## Prohibited use
The component must not assign official probability or impact, replace DFMEA/PFMEA, assign FMEA ratings, approve PPAP or Start of Production, or autonomously accept, merge, escalate, close or delete risks.

## Users
NPD Project Manager, APQP Coordinator, authorised risk owners and designated specialists.

## Limitations
Outputs may be wrong, incomplete, sensitive to wording, biased toward familiar professional writing, or affected by provider/model changes. Similar text does not prove duplicate meaning.

## Safeguards
Original text remains visible; findings include reasons; low-confidence cases are deferred; humans can correct or reject; all actions are logged; synthetic data is used for the prototype; manual fallback remains available.

## Change control
Re-evaluate after changes to model, prompt, taxonomy, schema, thresholds or target segment. Record version, evaluation result, owner and approval decision.
