## Supporting Presentation Artifacts

The final PowerPoint, executive brief, and enterprise risk workbook should be placed in this folder after their final content and source checks are complete.

## Presentation Position

The presentation must distinguish the model’s decision-support role from human decision authority. The ₹10 crore investment, 67% risk-reduction estimate, ₹710 crore potential loss avoidance, 7,000% indicative ROI, and sub-one-week payback are scenario projections until validated through controlled-pilot and financial evidence.
