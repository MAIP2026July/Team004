# 02 Product Strategy

This folder documents the product strategy for **RiskReview AI – Expanded Enterprise AI Risk Governance Framework**.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `01_Product_Strategy.md` | Strategic direction, differentiation, expanded scope, and outcomes |
| `02_Discovery_Plan.md` | Hypotheses, research participants, methods, and validation criteria |
| `03_Product_Vision_and_JTBD.md` | Product vision, stakeholders, and Jobs to Be Done |
| `04_AI_Opportunity_Matrix.md` | AI use-case prioritisation by value, suitability, readiness, complexity, and risk |
| `05_MVP_Scope.md` | MVP users, inputs, capabilities, workflow, success criteria, and exclusions |
| `06_MVP_to_Revenue_Roadmap.md` | Progression from discovery and MVP to paid pilot and enterprise scaling |

## Scope Position

Automotive AI and ECU systems are the primary application domain. Wind-turbine operations demonstrate transferability. Human decision authority, Responsible AI, Explainable AI, supplier governance, and continuous monitoring apply across both domains.
