# Product Vision and Jobs to Be Done

## 1. Product

**RiskReview AI**

RiskReview AI is a human-supervised AI copilot for reviewing programme-level risk registers in automotive component New Product Development and New Product Introduction programmes.

The initial product is focused on Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive component manufacturers managing programmes from Advanced Product Quality Planning (APQP) to Start of Production.

---

## 2. Product Vision

> Enable automotive programme teams to conduct more focused, evidence-based and decision-ready risk reviews by identifying incomplete, unclear, potentially related, stale or weakly controlled risk entries before the monthly review meeting, while keeping all consequential programme-risk decisions with authorised personnel.

The long-term vision is to help programme teams spend less time correcting risk-register content and more time making preventive decisions that protect customer milestones, launch readiness and accountable action closure.

---

## 3. Initial Customer Segment

- **Industry:** Automotive
- **Customer Segment:** Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive component manufacturers
- **Programme Type:** New Product Development and New Product Introduction
- **Lifecycle:** APQP to Start of Production
- **Primary Users:** NPD Project Manager and APQP Coordinator
- **Secondary Users:** Risk owners, Quality Managers, Supplier Quality Managers, Manufacturing Engineering, Tooling, Procurement, Logistics, Testing and Validation teams
- **Economic Buyer:** Programme leadership, PMO, Quality leadership or the automotive component manufacturer's business unit
- **Initial Workflow:** Monthly programme-risk and launch-readiness review

---

## 4. Primary Jobs to Be Done

### Main Jobs to Be Done Statement

> When we prepare the monthly programme-risk and launch-readiness review for a new automotive component programme, we want to identify unclear, incomplete, potentially duplicated, stale and weakly controlled risks before the meeting so that the cross-functional team can focus on preventive actions, customer milestones, accountable ownership and production-launch readiness.

### Functional Job

Prepare a complete and decision-ready programme risk register before the monthly review meeting.

### Emotional Job

Increase confidence that important risks, overdue actions and weak mitigations are visible before they affect customer or launch milestones.

### Social and Organisational Job

Demonstrate disciplined programme governance to programme leadership, quality management and automotive customers.

---

## 5. Stakeholder-Specific Jobs to Be Done

### NPD Project Manager

> When I prepare the programme-risk review, I want to see the risks requiring management attention, missing ownership, overdue actions and unclear mitigation so that I can run a decision-focused meeting and assign accountable actions.

### APQP Coordinator

> When I consolidate risks from different workstreams, I want to identify incomplete entries, stale evidence and potentially related risks so that the programme register is consistent and ready for launch-readiness governance.

### Risk Owner

> When I create or update a risk, I want clear feedback on missing information and weak cause-event-impact structure so that I can improve the entry without losing the original technical meaning.

### Quality or Supplier Quality Manager

> When I review programme risks, I want to understand whether quality, supplier-readiness, validation and PPAP-related concerns have current evidence and accountable actions so that unresolved issues are visible before customer milestones.

### Programme Leadership or PMO

> When I review programme health, I want a traceable summary of material risks, overdue actions, weak controls and human decisions so that I can assess launch readiness without relying on an unverified AI conclusion.

## 6. Customer Problem Being Solved

Automotive component programmes involve several interdependent workstreams, including customer requirements, design, prototype development, tooling, supplier readiness, process development, testing, quality planning, capacity, logistics, Production Part Approval Process and Start of Production.

Risk entries are created by different owners and may vary in quality. Common issues include:

- Missing owners, dates or supporting evidence
- Vague risk statements without clear cause, uncertain event and impact
- Similar risks recorded separately by different functions
- Mitigation actions that do not clearly address the stated cause
- Outdated evidence or overdue actions
- Risks that remain open without a clear trigger, contingency or decision

These issues can shift monthly review meetings from preventive decision-making to manual cleaning and interpretation of the register.

---

## 7. Desired Customer Outcome

RiskReview AI should help the programme team achieve the following outcome:

> A verified and decision-ready programme risk register, supported by clear evidence, accountable ownership and a focused monthly review agenda.

The product should improve preparation quality while preserving human authority over programme-risk decisions.

---

## 8. Product Promise

RiskReview AI will assist users by:

1. Checking mandatory fields, ownership, dates and overdue actions
2. Reviewing whether a risk statement has a clear cause-event-impact structure
3. Suggesting potentially related or duplicate risks for human comparison
4. Generating questions that challenge unclear mitigation actions
5. Flagging missing or stale supporting evidence
6. Supporting accept, edit, reject, link, merge or defer decisions
7. Generating a verified programme-review agenda
8. Recording the original entry, AI finding, human decision, reason, version and timestamp

---

## 9. Product Boundaries

RiskReview AI will not:

- Assign programme-risk probability or impact automatically
- Replace Design FMEA or Process FMEA
- Assign FMEA severity, occurrence or detection ratings
- Approve Production Part Approval Process submissions
- Approve Start of Production
- Automatically accept, escalate, close, merge or delete risks
- Create unsupported automotive programme facts
- Use confidential customer or supplier data in the prototype

All consequential decisions remain with authorised project personnel.

---

## 10. Why AI Is Appropriate

Deterministic rules are suitable for objective checks such as missing fields, overdue dates and invalid status values.

AI is useful where the product must interpret variable language and context, including:

- Whether a risk statement clearly separates cause, uncertain event and impact
- Whether two differently worded risks may describe the same underlying issue
- Whether a mitigation appears connected with the stated cause or impact
- Which clarification question may help the risk owner improve the entry

AI findings will be treated as review support, not verified programme facts.

---

## 11. Minimum Viable Product Vision

The first MVP will support:

- One fictional automotive component programme
- One standard risk-register format
- APQP to Start of Production
- English-language risk entries
- Synthetic programme-risk data
- Monthly pre-review preparation
- Human confirmation for every AI-generated finding

The MVP will test whether the product can improve risk-register readiness and reduce avoidable review effort without changing meaning, fabricating facts or taking control of consequential decisions.

---

## 12. Success Hypothesis

If RiskReview AI combines deterministic checks with human-supervised semantic review, then NPD Project Managers and APQP Coordinators should be able to prepare a more complete and decision-ready programme risk register with lower manual review effort.

This hypothesis remains provisional until validated through discovery interviews, baseline measurement, prototype testing and controlled evaluation.

---

## 13. Evidence Required

Before recommending a controlled pilot, the team must validate:

- The problem occurs repeatedly in the selected automotive segment
- Users spend material time correcting and interpreting risk-register entries
- The proposed findings are useful and understandable
- Human review effort remains manageable
- Confidentiality and trust controls are acceptable
- AI suggestions preserve the intended meaning
- The economic buyer sees enough value to consider a paid pilot
