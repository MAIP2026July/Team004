# AI Opportunity Matrix

## 1. Purpose

The AI Opportunity Matrix prioritises use cases according to enterprise value, AI suitability, data readiness, implementation complexity, governance risk, and required human oversight.

## 2. Scoring Scale

Each factor is scored from 1 to 5.

- **Value:** 1 = low, 5 = very high
- **AI suitability:** 1 = rules are sufficient, 5 = strong contextual or pattern-analysis need
- **Data readiness:** 1 = unavailable, 5 = reliable and accessible
- **Complexity:** 1 = low, 5 = very high
- **Governance risk:** 1 = low consequence, 5 = critical consequence

Scores are initial academic assessments and must be validated during discovery.

## 4. Prioritisation Logic

The MVP prioritises opportunities with meaningful user value, adequate data readiness, manageable complexity, and clear human verification. Critical operational-control use cases remain in shadow mode or specialist validation until evidence supports controlled deployment.

## 5. Recommended MVP Opportunities

1. Risk-record completeness and evidence checks
2. Related-risk suggestions with human confirmation
3. Supplier evidence-gap assessment
4. Explanation and uncertainty display
5. Human accept, reject, modify, and escalate workflow
6. Audit logging and governance thresholds

## 6. Opportunities Requiring Additional Evidence

- Fleet-scale edge-case mining
- OTA anomaly monitoring
- Wake-steering optimisation monitoring
- Automated incident consequence prediction
- Generative synthetic data for safety-relevant validation

These opportunities require representative data, domain-specific evaluation, security review, and expert approval.

## 7. Alignment with Professor Expectations

The matrix demonstrates structured AI opportunity selection, prioritisation, value-versus-risk analysis, MVP discipline, human oversight, and rejection of an unsafe autonomous decision use case.
